# ECC ORBIT — Project Brief and Requirements

## Purpose
ECC ORBIT Phase 1 examines an exploratory conversation about what two people can do with their talents and freely available technology to obtain feedback from a broad audience and, over time, build an income stream. The project should first understand what was actually said before deciding what the venture or documentary should become.

## Working hypothesis (project framing, not proven)
Two people may be able to combine their talents with freely accessible tools, create something valuable, expose it to public feedback, learn from that feedback, and progressively develop sustainable income. This is a hypothesis to test, not an established outcome or an agreed business plan.

## Questions to investigate
1. What talents, capabilities, interests, and constraints are actually expressed?
2. Which ideas are concretely proposed, and by whom?
3. What can be prototyped using genuinely free tools and no assumed capital?
4. How can public feedback be gathered ethically and meaningfully?
5. What encourages people to return, participate, or eventually pay?
6. Which income models are plausible, and what evidence would validate them?

## Phase 1 scope
Understand the recording and its ideas accurately; produce a timestamped Speaker A / Speaker B transcript only after access and privacy review; identify languages and code-switching; mark uncertainty and overlap; summarize ideas without inventing certainty; determine what should be researched next. The project is exploratory and should preserve that character.

## Requirements
- Keep source-derived statements distinct from editorial interpretation.
- Use neutral labels Speaker A and Speaker B; do not identify real-world participants.
- Do not claim a transcript, language, speaker attribution, idea, or business decision that has not been verified.
- Preserve the original audio unchanged if separately retained; derive working copies for processing.
- No external transcription or upload without explicit informed authorization and a privacy review.
- Treat zero-cost tooling as a requirement to verify, including limits, privacy terms, and hidden costs; do not label a service free without checking.
- Keep any public use on hold until all participants have reviewed the proposed use and given informed permission, and applicable legal questions are assessed.

## Desired later outputs (not yet completed)
Verified transcript with timestamps and uncertainty markers; idea inventory; evidence-based assessment of possible prototypes; ethical feedback experiment; and only then a separately approved public-facing documentary or content plan.
