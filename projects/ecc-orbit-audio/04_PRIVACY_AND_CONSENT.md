## Known fact
The user stated that only one of the two individuals knew the conversation was being recorded. This is a sensitive privacy condition. It does not establish the other person’s consent to record, private analysis, transcription, processing by AI, quotation, or publication.

## Binding project safeguards for continuation
- Keep the original private and access-limited; do not include it in this transfer archive.
- Do not identify either participant. Use Speaker A and Speaker B.
- Do not upload the audio or transcript to an external service unless the user has explicitly authorized that processing after reviewing current privacy and cost conditions.
- Keep publication on hold. Permission to record, permission to analyze, permission to publish text, and permission to publish an identifiable voice are separate decisions.
- Avoid exposing private or sensitive statements unnecessarily, even in internal notes.
- Do not treat anonymity, editing, or a narrated reconstruction as automatically removing ethical or legal risk.
- If permissions cannot be obtained, use only material that can be responsibly described without revealing identity or private details; reassess before any external use.
